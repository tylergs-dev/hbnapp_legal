Use this page if you want us to delete your Hushabye app account or personal data. You can also delete your account from Settings in the app.

What happens next

Submitting the form opens your email app with a message addressed to tylerg.dev@gmail.com and apps@hushabyenursery.org. Send that email to complete your request. We will follow up using the address you provide.

If you request account deletion, we retain the account and associated data for 30 days. During that period you may sign in again to cancel. After 30 days, the account and associated data are permanently deleted. See our Privacy Policy for details.
